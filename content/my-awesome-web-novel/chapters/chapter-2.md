# Chapter 2: The Journey Begins

Aria clutched the ancient scroll tightly as she left the library, the weight of destiny pressing upon her shoulders. The city of Eldoria bustled around her—merchants hawking wares, children playing in the streets, guards patrolling their routes—but none of them knew the storm that was coming.

She needed guidance. The scroll had mentioned a mentor, someone who could help her understand the powers awakening within her. According to the ancient text, she should seek out the Oracle of the Crystal Caves, hidden deep in the Whispering Mountains.

The journey would be dangerous. Bandits roamed the roads, and the mountains themselves were treacherous. But Aria had no choice. The darkness the prophecy spoke of was already stirring—she could feel it in her bones.

With a deep breath, she set out from the city gates, the scroll safely tucked in her satchel. Little did she know that her first companion was already watching from the shadows, drawn by the same mysterious energy that had awakened the ancient runes.

The adventure had begun, and there was no turning back.
