# Chapter 1: The Forgotten Village

In the shadowed valleys of Eldoria, where ancient forests whispered secrets to the wind, lay the forgotten village of Thornewood. It was a place untouched by the wars of kings and the machinations of mages, a quiet hamlet nestled between towering oaks and mist-shrouded hills.

Elara was the village blacksmith's daughter, a young woman of eighteen summers with hair like raven wings and eyes that sparkled with unspoken dreams. She had always felt different from the others—able to sense the subtle shifts in the earth, to hear the distant calls of creatures that no one else could perceive.

One fateful morning, as the first light of dawn pierced the canopy, a terrible roar echoed through the valley. The ground trembled, and the villagers rushed from their homes in terror. From the eastern hills descended a shadow, massive and winged—a dragon, thought extinct for centuries.

But this was no ordinary beast. Its scales shimmered with an unnatural light, and in its eyes burned a intelligence that chilled Elara to her core. As the villagers fled, Elara stood transfixed, feeling a strange connection to the creature.

The dragon landed in the village square, its claws tearing into the earth. "I seek the heir," it rumbled, its voice like thunder. "The one who bears the mark of Axions."

Elara's heart pounded. She had always wondered about the strange birthmark on her wrist—a glowing rune that appeared only in moonlight. Was this the mark the dragon spoke of?

As the dragon's gaze fell upon her, Elara knew her life would never be the same. The forgotten village had awakened something ancient, and the path of destiny lay open before her.
